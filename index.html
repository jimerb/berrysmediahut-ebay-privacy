Privacy Policy — BerrysMediahut eBay Helper

Last updated: August 12, 2026

The BerrysMediahut eBay Helper is a private tool used solely by the account owner to research current eBay listings and prepare unpublished eBay listing drafts.

Information the helper uses

When authorized by the account owner, the helper accesses only the eBay data needed to perform the requested action, such as current listing information and seller inventory or draft-listing details.

Storage and sharing

The helper does not maintain a database of eBay customer data, order data, or eBay listing data. It does not sell, rent, or share eBay data with third parties.

Security

eBay application credentials and authorization tokens are kept private and are used only to operate this helper for the account owner.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner through the BerrysMediahut eBay account.
